## Reproduction

```powershell
.\opacha.ps1 :lwjgl3:balancePlaytest -PcampaignLab -PlabName=campaign
.\opacha.ps1 :lwjgl3:balancePlaytest -PlabName=random
.\opacha.ps1 :lwjgl3:balancePlaytest -PcampaignLab -PlabName=holdout '-PlabLevels=19,21,48' -PlabSeedStart=3 -PlabSeeds=4
```

A match stops at a mode winner, human elimination, or 300 game seconds. `winner=null` before 300 seconds means human elimination before the remaining AI decided the winner; it is not a timeout. Capture counts are sampled every half game second and can miss a very fast capture/recapture between samples. All match rows, including failures, remain in the CSVs.
