# Playtest evidence

These are scripted games of the actual model, with rendering and sound disabled during the accelerated simulation loop. They are not results from recruited players.

| File | Games | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| campaign.csv | 672 | All 48 maps × seven policies × two decision-error seeds |
| random.csv | 224 | Four modes × four map seeds × two starting seats × seven policies, hard difficulty |
| holdout.csv | 84 | Maps 19, 21 and 48 × four additional error seeds × seven policies |
| baseline.csv | 192 | Earlier six-policy diagnostic sweep; policy revisions prevent a causal before/after comparison |

The final 980 games use the same policy implementation and production rules. Authored maps keep their own difficulty and visible starting forces. Campaign adaptive difficulty is disabled for reproducibility; the shipped adaptive setting can slow the opponents after losses. Extra seeds change decision errors and AI evaluation phase, not the authored geometry.

`HumanStylePlayer.java` defines each strategy and its action limits. `BalancePlaytest.java` writes every match, including eliminated players and unfinished games. `CampaignChecks.java` validates all maps with the real importer and editor model. `TacticsChecks.java` tests prices, combat, regeneration, diplomacy and AI tool decisions.

[Results and reproduction commands](../balance-metrics.md) are generated by `scripts/analyze-playtests.py`. [Interpretation and suggested experiments](../balance.md) distinguish tested mechanics from proposals.
